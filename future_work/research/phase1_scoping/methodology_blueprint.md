# Methodology Blueprint
**Agent:** `research_architect_agent` | **Phase:** 1 (Scoping) | **Date:** 2026-08-17
**Consumes:** `rq_brief.md`

---

## Research Paradigm

**Selected:** Post-positivist experimental (controlled manipulation with statistical inference),
operating on a *black-box behavioural* epistemology.

**Justification.** The RQ is causal and manipulable: format is assigned by the experimenter, all
other properties of the stimulus descend from a single canonical ancestor, and the outcome is
observable. What the paradigm must concede is that the mechanism is not directly observable — the
target model is closed-weights and API-only, so internal states are unavailable in principle. The
design therefore infers mechanism the way psychophysics does: by constructing contrasts in which
exactly one thing differs and reading the mechanism off the behavioural difference. XAI here is
not introspection; it is systematic stimulus perturbation. That distinction is load-bearing and is
stated explicitly wherever "explanation" appears in the report.

---

## Method

**Type:** Quantitative, within-item repeated-measures experiment.

**Specific method:** A fully crossed 4 (format) × 2 (task) × 3 (repeat) factorial over 50 fixed
items, plus a nested black-box attribution sub-experiment on a stratified item subset under two
formats.

**Justification.** Every item appears in every format, so item difficulty, speaker identity, and
sentence content are held constant *within* the unit of analysis; the format effect is estimated
from within-item differences rather than between-group comparison. This is the strongest available
design given n=50 and it eliminates the dominant nuisance variance (speaker and sentence) by
construction. Repeats capture API sampling variability, which must be separated from format
effects because both manifest as changed labels.

### The three planned contrasts

| Contrast | What differs | What it identifies |
|----------|--------------|--------------------|
| `roundtrip_wav` − `ref` | Waveform (AAC coding loss); container held constant (both WAV) | **Signal effect** — pure codec degradation |
| `mp4_aac64` − `roundtrip_wav` | Container/bitstream; **decoded waveform held constant** | **Container effect** — routing, decoder, or ingestion-path differences |
| `mp4_aac64` − `ref` | Both | **Total format effect** (= signal + container, additively) |

`mp3_64` is a second lossy operating point with no round-trip control; it contributes to SQ1 only
and cannot be decomposed. This asymmetry is stated rather than papered over.

**The decomposition is the design's central claim to novelty and its central fragility.** It holds
only if the `roundtrip_wav` waveform genuinely equals the decoded `mp4_aac64` waveform
(assumption A2). Verification is a blocking gate — see Validity, V1.

## Data Strategy

**Data type:** Primary (model responses generated by this study) over secondary stimuli (an
existing public corpus).

**Sources**
- **Stimuli:** CREMA-D (Crowd-sourced Emotional Multimodal Actors Dataset) — 50 clips already
  sampled into `data/raw/`, re-derived into `data/stimuli/` as four conditions each.
  Fixed-lexicon sentences; the fixed lexicon is load-bearing (it makes the transcription task's
  WER comparable across items) and the corpus must not be swapped.
- **Model:** Google AI Studio, `gemini-flash-latest` → `gemini-3.6-flash`, captured per call.
- **Audio submitted as raw base64 bytes**, never as filename or URL — a filename would leak the
  condition into the prompt and silently destroy the manipulation.

**Sampling**
- Item sampling: stratified by emotion label across the 6-way lexicon, and diversified across
  speaker IDs, from the CREMA-D pool. Fixed and frozen before any model call (already done;
  recorded in `data/manifest.parquet`).
- XAI subset: 20 items drawn stratified by emotion from the label-agreement subset (items where
  `ref` and `mp4_aac64` return the same modal label), because SQ3 is by construction about items
  whose *accuracy* outcome is unchanged. Subset selection is therefore **outcome-dependent and
  must be declared as such** — it is not a random subsample and no accuracy claim may be made on it.

**Time frame**
- Single pilot window, August 2026. Free-tier quota may spread the grid over several days; the
  runner is resumable and idempotent (content-hash call IDs).
- **Consequence:** wall-clock spread introduces possible API-side model drift. Mitigation in
  Validity, V4.

### Call budget

| Arm | Composition | Calls |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| Main grid | 50 items × 4 formats × 2 tasks × 3 repeats | 1,200 |
| Prompt sensitivity | 50 × {`ref`,`mp4_aac64`} × {`emotion_v2`,`emotion_v3`} × 3 | 600 |
| **XAI attribution** | 20 items × 2 formats × 16 masks × 3 repeats | 1,920 |
| **XAI stability floor** | 20 items × `ref` × 16 masks × 3 repeats (independent second pass) | 960 |

At the smoke-run observed cost (~$0.0004/call) this projects to **≈ $2.00**, well inside the
$25 hard ceiling enforced in the runner. Wall-clock, not cost, is the binding constraint.

## Analytical Framework

### Technique

Mixed-effects modelling for the performance questions; rank-based attribution comparison with an
explicit noise floor for the explanation question; equivalence testing wherever a null is the
informative result.

### Steps

**Step 1 — Fidelity verification (blocking gate).**
Before any inferential analysis: decode `mp4_aac64` and compare sample-wise against
`roundtrip_wav`; report max absolute sample deviation and per-item SNR. Also report objective
degradation of each lossy condition against `ref` (SNR, log-spectral distance, and a perceptual
measure such as PESQ or ViSQOL where applicable at 16 kHz). If `mp4_aac64` and `roundtrip_wav`
are not numerically equivalent, **SQ2 and SQ3 are unanswerable as designed** and the study
reduces to SQ1 — this outcome must be reported, not repaired by relaxing the criterion.

**Step 2 — SQ1, accuracy.**
Binomial GLMM: `correct ~ format + task + (1 | item) + (1 | speaker)`, `ref` as reference level.
Report marginal accuracy per format with bootstrap CIs, odds ratios per contrast, and Holm
correction across the three planned contrasts. WER for the transcription task analysed with a
parallel linear mixed model on logit-transformed WER (or a beta regression), reported separately —
the two tasks are not pooled.

**Step 3 — SQ2, decomposition.**
Estimate signal and container effects from the contrasts above. Because the *expected and most
informative* container result is a null, a non-significant test is not sufficient: run a
**two-one-sided-tests (TOST) equivalence test** against a pre-declared smallest effect size of
interest (Δ = 3 accuracy points, ~1 item in 33 flipping). Only a passed equivalence test licenses
"the container does not matter"; a non-significant, non-equivalent result is reported as
inconclusive.

**Step 4 — XAI attribution.**
For each item × format:
- **Temporal occlusion.** Segment the clip into K = 10 equal-duration windows. For each window,
  replace it with speech-shaped noise at matched short-term loudness, with 10 ms raised-cosine
  edge ramps (avoiding click artefacts). Submit the masked clip R = 3 times.
- **Spectral occlusion.** Apply a band-stop filter over B = 6 bands spanning 0–8 kHz
  (0–250, 250–500, 500–1k, 1–2k, 2–4k, 4–8k Hz), one at a time. Submit R = 3 times.
- **Attribution score.** No token log-probabilities are exposed, so importance is measured as
  *label-flip rate*: `a_j = 1 − (# repeats returning the unmasked modal label) / R`. This yields a
  16-dimensional attribution vector per item per format. The measure is coarse (R = 3 gives four
  possible values) — an acknowledged precision limit, mitigated by aggregating across items rather
  than interpreting single-item maps.
- **Mask-artefact control.** Two null masks per item (a leading-silence window and a band above
  the speech energy range) estimate the flip rate attributable to the masking operation itself.
  Attribution scores are interpreted relative to this floor.

**Step 5 — SQ3, explanation divergence.**
- Across-format similarity: `S_across = ρ(a_ref, a_mp4)` (Spearman) and top-3 Jaccard overlap.
- **Within-format noise floor:** `S_within = ρ(a_ref, a_ref′)` from the independent second pass on
  `ref`. This is the crux of the analysis. An explanation shift exists only if
  `S_across` is reliably *below* `S_within`.
- Test: Wilcoxon signed-rank on the paired per-item difference `S_within − S_across`, item as the
  unit, with a bootstrap CI on the median difference.
- **Pre-declared interpretation grid:**

  | Accuracy (SQ1/SQ2) | Explanation (SQ3) | Reading |
  |---|---|---|
  | Degraded | Diverged | Ordinary degradation; XAI localises *where* |
  | Degraded | Stable | Uniform signal loss, same evidence base, less of it |
  | **Unchanged** | **Diverged** | **The headline dissociation — benchmarks under-report format sensitivity** |
  | Unchanged | Stable | Format-robust at this operating point; licenses format-agnostic pipelines |

  All four cells are reportable outcomes. Declaring them in advance is what prevents the study
  from becoming a search for the interesting cell.

**Step 6 — Conditional secondary analysis.**
If the main grid yields ≥ 15 items that flip label between `ref` and `mp4_aac64`, test whether
`ref`-side attribution concentration (e.g. entropy of `a_ref`) predicts flip probability. If
fewer, the analysis is skipped and reported as skipped — the threshold is declared now, not after
seeing the count.

### Tools

Python 3.11 (`.venv`), `ffmpeg` (pinned version recorded) for all transforms and masking,
`pandas`/`pyarrow` for the results store, `statsmodels` / `pymer4` for mixed models, `scipy` for
rank statistics and bootstrap, `jiwer` for WER, `matplotlib` for figures. All configs live in
`configs/`; all analysis is scripted, no manual steps.

## Validity Criteria

| Criterion | Strategy to ensure |
|-----------|--------------------|
| **V1 — Signal identity (construct validity of SQ2)** | Numerically verify `roundtrip_wav` ≡ decode(`mp4_aac64`) before interpretation. **Blocking gate.** Report the actual deviation, not a pass/fail claim. |
| **V2 — Common ancestry (internal validity)** | Every condition is generated from one canonical `ref` (16 kHz mono PCM16, EBU R128 normalised), never from the CREMA-D source independently. Prevents divergent resampling or loudness paths from masquerading as format effects. |
| **V3 — Condition concealment** | Audio submitted as raw base64 with a MIME type only; no filename, path, or URL. Prompts are byte-identical across formats. |
| **V4 — Drift control** | Randomise call order so formats are interleaved within and across items rather than run in blocks; record `model_version` and timestamp per call; include timestamp as a covariate in a sensitivity model. If `model_version` changes mid-run, treat the run as stratified and report both strata. |
| **V5 — Sampling-noise separation** | 3 repeats per cell at fixed decoding settings; report per-cell repeat agreement. If repeat disagreement approaches the size of the format effect, increase repeats before interpreting SQ3 (assumption A4). |
| **V6 — Attribution faithfulness** | Mask-artefact null controls; ramped mask edges; loudness-matched filler; attribution interpreted only above the null-mask floor. |
| **V7 — Explanation-shift specificity** | The within-format stability floor (`S_within`) is mandatory. Without it a divergence measurement is uninterpretable, because attribution maps built from a stochastic API are unstable even under no manipulation. |
| **V8 — Prompt robustness** | `emotion_v2` / `emotion_v3` arm on `ref` and `mp4_aac64` tests whether the format effect survives rewording. A format effect that appears under only one prompt is a prompt artefact. |
| **V9 — Analytic transparency** | Contrasts, models, equivalence bounds, subset rules, and the conditional-analysis threshold are all declared before the full grid is run (preregistration below). |
| **V10 — Reproducibility** | Content-hash call IDs, resumable idempotent runner, raw responses persisted per call, ffmpeg and library versions pinned, seeds recorded. |

## Limitations (By Design)

- **Single model, single corpus, single bitrate.** External validity is deliberately traded for
  internal validity. Findings are about *this* model at *this* operating point; the design is
  offered as a reusable protocol, not a general claim about audio LLMs. Mitigation: publish the
  protocol and configs so replication across models is cheap.
- **Acted emotion.** CREMA-D actors portray emotions; results speak to acoustic-format sensitivity,
  not to emotion inference in the wild. No claim about ecological emotion recognition is made.
- **Coarse attribution resolution.** Label-flip rate at R = 3 is a 4-level measure. Mitigated by
  item-level aggregation; a higher-R replication is named as the obvious extension.
- **No log-probabilities.** Confidence is proxied by repeat agreement. If the API later exposes
  logprobs, the same design yields a strictly better attribution signal.
- **Perturbation-based XAI is not mechanism.** It identifies which regions the model's output
  depends on, not what the model computes. The report must not slide from "depends on" to
  "attends to".
- **Occlusion-based attribution can be confounded by out-of-distribution masks.** The null-mask
  control bounds this but does not eliminate it.
- **The XAI subset is outcome-selected.** It is chosen for label agreement, so it is not
  representative of the item pool and supports no accuracy inference.

## Ethical Considerations

- **Corpus.** CREMA-D is publicly released for research with consented actor participants; no new
  human-subjects data is collected and no re-identification is attempted.
- **Dual use.** Speech emotion recognition has surveillance and workplace-monitoring applications.
  This study measures *format sensitivity of a model*, and its findings should not be presented as
  validating emotion inference. A Responsible Use Statement is required in the report (advisory,
  not blocking).
- **Model terms.** Calls comply with Google AI Studio terms; no attempt is made to extract weights,
  circumvent safety systems, or reverse-engineer the ingestion pipeline beyond legitimate
  black-box behavioural measurement.
- **Credentials.** API keys live in a gitignored `.env`. Both keys used in this project were shared
  in plaintext during setup and **must be rotated** at pilot end.
- **AI disclosure.** The research pipeline is AI-assisted (agentic literature search, drafting, and
  analysis scaffolding under human direction). Disclosed in the report per Phase 5 ethics review.

These supersede the corresponding text above. See `da_checkpoint1.md` and `feasibility_addendum.md`.

| Ref | Amendment |
|-----|-----------|
| **C1** | Step 3's Δ = 3 points is **withdrawn**. Δ is computed from observed `ref`↔`mp4_aac64` discordance as the smallest bound achievable at 80% power, declared before the main grid, and reported as a limitation. "Inconclusive" is a pre-authorised SQ2 outcome with pre-written phrasing; a non-significant, non-equivalent result is never reported as "no container effect". |
| **M1** | Step 1's fidelity gate must **align by cross-correlation before computing any deviation metric**, and report recovered AAC encoder delay and duration difference per item as data. A delay-compensated `ref` variant is added if the shift is non-trivial. |
| **M2** | XAI repeats raised from R = 3 to **R = 7** (eight attribution levels). Tie fractions reported; a minimum acceptable `S_within` is pre-registered, below which attribution is declared too noisy to interpret. |
| **M3** | The transcription arm is reframed in advance as a **positive control for intelligibility preservation**, not a second hypothesis test. Floor-level WER across all conditions is a reportable finding (format effect is paralinguistic-specific). |
| **M4** | **`roundtrip_wav_mp3` is added** to `configs/transforms.yaml`, making the decomposition symmetric across both codecs. |
| **M5** | A null on SQ2 is reported as evidence for a **disjunction** (container irrelevant *or* ingestion decodes early), never for the first disjunct alone. Optional discriminating probe: a container with intact payload but altered container-level metadata. |
| **Minor** | Call sequence position recorded and checked as a covariate. A wall-clock budget and stopping rule are added. FINER Novelty (4/5) is treated as a hypothesis for Phase 2 to falsify. |
| **F1–F3** | Gate **G0** (reference-accuracy floor ≥ 40%) precedes all other gates; billing must be enabled before the grid; Feasible re-scored 2/5 pending G0. |
| **C2** (DA CP2) | New gate **G0b — response degeneracy check**, run on the G0 sample before any XAI call. Compute the entropy of the label distribution across items and across masks. **If the modal label is constant for >30% of items across all 16 masks, flip-rate attribution is declared inapplicable** and the pre-specified replacement takes effect: a **forced binary contrast** (gold label vs. a confusable alternative) that guarantees a non-degenerate response distribution. Rationale: under a degenerate response the attribution vector is constant, Spearman ρ is *undefined* (not low), and SQ3 returns an all-zero table that reads as "perfectly stable explanations". The analysis code must **raise** on a constant-vector correlation, never emit `nan` into a results table. |
| **M8** (DA CP2) | `S_within` is promoted from internal control to **reported primary result** — the run-to-run stability of black-box occlusion attribution on a stochastic commercial audio LLM, a quantity with no published reference value. |
| **M6** (DA CP2) | Evidence base extended with counter-evidence (Zhang et al., 2026; Hu et al., 2025; Lee et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2026). Whether this model is lexically dominant on this corpus is an **open empirical question that G0 answers**, not a literature-established expectation. |
